Calibration: set media volume to maximum, turn off sound effects, set a channel to 100 %, and measure it with a true-RMS meter. New calcoef = old calcoef × 0.333 ÷ measured V. Each channel has its own calcoef (default 1.000), and the values are remembered on this device. Sliders reach 105 % (about 0.99 Vpp, the 1 Vpp limit).
